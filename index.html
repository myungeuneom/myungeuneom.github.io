About

My research focuses on developing practical algorithms with theoretical guarantees for large-scale optimization, particularly in transportation and logistics. I am also broadly interested in decision making under uncertainty, especially for matching markets and online platforms.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from Georgia Tech, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Alejandro Toriello. I spent two summers interning at Amazon, on the middle mile planning and routing optimization science team (mmPros) and on the inbound placement team, and a summer at UC Berkeley working on dynamic matching problems with Prof. Chiwei Yan. Prior to Georgia Tech, I received my B.S. and M.S. in Industrial and Management Engineering from Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH), where I worked on scheduling and transportation problems in manufacturing and healthcare.
